Time & Frequency Rulers Demo

Modular 2D Canvas coordinate scales: Vertical FrequencyRuler (supporting Mel, Linear, and Log scales) and Horizontal TimeRuler (Ticks and Boxes programs) locked in perfect sync with the WebGL2 Spectrogram.

💡 Navigation Shortcuts: Drag or scroll directly on the Frequency Ruler to pan/zoom frequency. On the Spectrogram, use standard Drag / Wheel for Time, Ctrl+Wheel to Zoom Time, hold Shift+Wheel / Shift+Drag to Scroll Frequency, and Ctrl+Shift+Wheel to Zoom Frequency!
