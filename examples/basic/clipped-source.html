Clipped Audio Source & Dynamic Bounds

Enforce audio clip limits at the source and playback level with clipStart and clipEnd. Visualization and audio playback stay strictly bounded within the clip. Change clips dynamically with scope.setClipBounds() without reloading the source.

Clip Presets:
Clip: [0.00s, 20.00s] Playhead: 0.00s

Source-Level Protection: STFT computation and audio playback are restricted to the active clip.
Playback Enforced: Audio automatically starts at clipStart, pauses at clipEnd, and seeks outside the clip are clamped.
